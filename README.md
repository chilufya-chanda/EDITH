# EDITH
### Embedded Detective & Intelligent Tracking Hub

**An AI-powered investigative intelligence platform designed to help locate missing people, connect evidence, and support faster, more informed investigations.**

EDITH is an ambitious technology project focused on transforming missing-person investigations through artificial intelligence, computer vision, geospatial intelligence, and connected investigative data.

The platform envisions a future where information from missing-person reports, public sightings, authorized surveillance systems, and other relevant sources can be brought together into a unified investigative environment.

Rather than relying on isolated reports and manual information gathering, EDITH aims to help investigators discover meaningful connections between evidence, identify potential leads, and develop a clearer understanding of a missing person's possible whereabouts.

> Turning fragmented information into connected intelligence — and connected intelligence into the possibility of bringing someone home.

---

## The Problem

Missing-person investigations can involve large amounts of fragmented information.

Photographs, witness reports, CCTV footage, location records, and investigative notes may exist across different systems and organizations. Manually reviewing and connecting this information can be time-consuming and difficult.

EDITH explores how intelligent software can help reduce this complexity by organizing investigative information and assisting with evidence analysis.

## The Vision

EDITH is being designed as a centralized investigative intelligence ecosystem with the ability to:

- Create and manage structured missing-person cases.
- Analyze identifying characteristics and visual evidence.
- Receive and organize public sightings.
- Correlate sightings with locations and timestamps.
- Support geographic search planning.
- Connect relevant evidence into investigative timelines.
- Provide investigators with a unified operational dashboard.

The long-term vision is to build a scalable platform that can support missing-person investigations in Zambia and potentially beyond.

## Core Features

### 1. Intelligent Case Management

Create detailed digital case profiles containing:

- Missing-person photographs
- Physical descriptions and identifying marks
- Clothing and appearance details
- Last-known location
- Date and time last seen
- Relevant case information and evidence

Each case serves as a central record that can be updated as new information becomes available.

### 2. AI-Powered Visual Intelligence

EDITH aims to incorporate computer vision and AI-assisted visual analysis to help identify potential matches between missing-person profiles and reported sightings.

The system may analyze visual characteristics such as facial features, clothing, appearance, and other distinguishing details.

AI-generated matches are intended to serve as investigative leads requiring human review, not as definitive proof of identity.

### 3. Geospatial Intelligence

EDITH is designed to combine geographic information with reported sightings and timestamps.

This could help investigators:

- Identify areas associated with relevant sightings.
- Visualize possible movement patterns.
- Examine relationships between locations.
- Support more coordinated search operations.

### 4. Connected Evidence Intelligence

The platform's long-term architecture envisions integrations with authorized CCTV networks, approved location-data sources, and relevant investigative systems.

The goal is to make it easier to correlate observations from different sources and organize them into a coherent investigative timeline.

### 5. AI Case Twin

The **AI Case Twin** is one of EDITH's advanced concepts.

It represents a missing-person case as an evolving digital intelligence model containing identifying information, visual references, evidence, sightings, locations, and investigative history.

This concept aims to help investigators understand a case as a connected system rather than a collection of unrelated records.

### 6. Investigator Command Center

A centralized dashboard is envisioned for authorized investigators to manage cases, review evidence, assess potential matches, monitor reported sightings, and coordinate investigative activity.

The dashboard is intended to provide a clear operational overview of active cases and emerging leads.

## Technology Direction

EDITH explores the integration of several technology domains:

- Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
- Computer Vision
- Geospatial Intelligence
- Evidence Correlation
- Data Analysis and Visualization
- Secure Backend Systems
- Real-Time Information Processing
- Digital Case Modeling

The architecture is intended to be modular, allowing individual capabilities to evolve independently as the project develops.

## Responsible Intelligence

EDITH is being developed around the principle that powerful investigative technology must be used responsibly.

The project recognizes the importance of:

- Privacy and data protection
- Authorized access to sensitive information
- Secure handling of investigative evidence
- Human verification of AI-generated leads
- Accountability and responsible use
- Preventing unauthorized surveillance or misuse

AI should assist investigators, not replace human judgment.

## Project Status

EDITH is an evolving project and long-term technology vision.

The capabilities described in this README represent the project's intended direction and design concepts. Individual features, AI models, and external integrations may be under development, experimental, or not yet implemented.

The project will continue to evolve as its architecture, functionality, and technical capabilities are developed.

## Long-Term Objective

The ultimate goal of EDITH is to create a powerful, responsible, and scalable investigative intelligence platform that helps transform how missing-person cases are approached.

By connecting evidence, analyzing information intelligently, and supporting coordinated investigations, EDITH aims to contribute to a future where finding missing people becomes faster, more organized, and more effective.

**EDITH — Intelligence that helps bring people home.**
